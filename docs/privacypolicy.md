Elite Bot ("Elite Bot", "we", "us" or "our") operates https://elite-bot.com (the "site") and the Elite Bot Discord bot (the "bot"). This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have.

**Data controller:** Elite Bot, United Kingdom. You can contact us through the [support Discord server](https://discord.elite-bot.com) or by email at thatguyjacobee@gmail.com.

## Data we collect

Elite Bot is a Discord bot, so most of the data we handle comes from Discord. We collect and store:

- **Server and channel identifiers:** guild IDs, channel IDs and role IDs needed to provide features such as welcome messages, logging, join roles, role menus, verification, counting games, XP, giveaways, member counts, join-to-create voice channels and FiveM/RedM status updates.
- **User identifiers and feature data:** Discord user IDs linked to XP totals and levels, warnings (including the reason and the moderator who issued them), temporary bans (including the user ID and expiry time), giveaway entries and whether an entry won, and the last user to count in a counting game.
- **Bot configuration:** the settings each server administrator chooses, such as channel selections, role selections, messages, titles and toggles. Custom embeds and button messages created by administrators are stored so the bot can post or update them.
- **FiveM/RedM status details:** the server's cfx.re join code or URL and the status message details configured by the server administrator.

We do not store your messages. See "Message content" below.

## Message content

Elite Bot's commands are slash commands, so it does not read message text to receive commands.

Message text is processed only when a server administrator has enabled a feature that needs it:

- **Anti-spam and anti-similarity:** the bot compares a member's new message with their previous message to detect repeated or near-duplicate spam. The previous message is held in memory only, for as long as the feature needs it, and is lost when the bot restarts. It is not written to our database.
- **Counting game:** the bot checks that a message contains the next number in the game. It stores the current count and the last user ID, not the message.
- **Message logging:** when logging is enabled, the text of a deleted or edited message is posted to the log channel chosen by that server so its moderators can review it. The bot does not keep its own copy. That posted message is stored by Discord in the server's channel and is controlled by the server, not by us.

We do not store message content in our database or in our backups, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, analytics or AI training.

## Website data

When you visit the site, we use Google Analytics to understand how it is used. Google Analytics may collect your IP address, browser type, device information, the pages you visit, the time and date of your visit and similar usage data, and may set cookies. We also use your browser's local storage to remember site preferences such as your theme.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect parts of the site. You can also use Google's opt-out browser add-on if you do not want Google Analytics to collect your data.

## How we use data

We use the data described above to:

- provide the features a server administrator has enabled;
- operate, maintain and secure the bot and the site;
- respond to support requests, bug reports and suggestions;
- understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

Our lawful bases are our legitimate interests in operating and improving Elite Bot, performing the service a server administrator has asked us to provide, and, where required, consent (for example for non-essential cookies). Server administrators are responsible for making sure they have the permissions and notices needed for the features they enable in their server.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data for advertising. We share data only where needed to run the service:

- **Discord:** all bot interactions and posted messages are handled by Discord under its own privacy policy.
- **Hosting providers:** our bot, database and site run on servers provided by hosting and infrastructure providers.
- **Top.gg:** we automatically post aggregate server counts to our Top.gg listing. This does not include message content or member data.
- **Google Analytics:** as described above, for site usage statistics.
- **Legal requirements:** we may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights, safety or security of Elite Bot, its users or others.

The FiveM/RedM status feature makes requests to cfx.re and to the server address configured by the administrator so it can display live status information.

## Retention

We keep server configuration and feature data for as long as the bot is in the server or as long as needed for the feature to work. If you ask us to delete data, we will remove it unless we need to keep it for a legal reason.

We keep weekly database backups and retain four of them, so data may remain in backups for up to about four weeks before it is overwritten.

Messages posted by the bot into a server's channels, including log messages, are stored by Discord and are controlled by that server. We cannot delete them remotely.

## Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you;
- ask us to correct inaccurate data;
- ask us to delete your data;
- restrict or object to how we use your data;
- ask for a copy of your data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent where we rely on it.

To make a request, join the [support Discord server](https://discord.elite-bot.com) or email thatguyjacobee@gmail.com. Please include the relevant Discord user or server ID so we can find the data. We may ask for reasonable proof that you control the account or server.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at https://ico.org.uk.

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

Elite Bot is not intended for anyone under the age of 13. Discord requires users to be at least 13, and the age may be higher in some countries. We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## International transfers

Discord, our hosting providers and Google may process data outside the UK. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards for the transfer as required by data protection law.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will change when we do. If we make a material change, we will post a notice on the site or in the support Discord server.
